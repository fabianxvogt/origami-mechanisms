02 / ASSEMBLY GUIDANCE

01

Print at 100% / measure the bar

Use the exported 10 mm scale bar. Do not use “fit to page”; measure the result before any paper test.

↗
02

Cut boundary, score creases

Cut the outer quadrilateral only. Score all four center creases, then precrease lightly on the marked M/V side.

↗
03

Start flat and close together

Start near q=0°. Bring the four panels in together while keeping O and all endpoints aligned. Stop before 160° if paper resists.
